Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Tsinghua University

I investigate multiphase flow and thermochemical conversion in solid waste and biomass valorization—from droplet dynamics to process-scale simulation—using experiments, computational fluid dynamics, and process modeling.

我研究固体废弃物与生物质资源化中的多相流与热化学转化，从液滴动力学到过程尺度模拟，结合实验、计算流体力学与过程建模。
